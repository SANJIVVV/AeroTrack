# Image attributions

All photographs are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) and are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free to use, including commercially, without permission; attribution is not required but is given here and on the pages that show them. Each photo's Unsplash page was opened and showed "Free to use under the Unsplash License" (not Unsplash+) when checked on 20–21 October 2026.

Images are not stored in the repository. They load at runtime from `images.unsplash.com`; if they cannot load, the UI shows a gradient instead. The source of truth is `client/js/lib/photos.js`.

**Airline photos.** An airline card uses a photo of that airline's aircraft only where the photo's own Unsplash title or description identifies the airline. Air India Express and SpiceJet have no checked photo, so their cards show a generic airliner photo labelled "Illustrative photo". Photos show aircraft liveries as they appear in public; no airline logos are bundled or redrawn, and brand marks in the app are plain text. AeroTrack is not affiliated with any airline.

**Illustrations.** The hero airliner, runway scene, cloud layers and icons are original SVG/CSS artwork made for this project (icons: [Lucide](https://lucide.dev), ISC licence).

**Not used.** The reference site trustvault-5lgg.onrender.com could not be inspected (its robots.txt disallows automated access), so nothing was taken from it.
